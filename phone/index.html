What the measurements say

The width is fine. Nothing overflows sideways at 393, 375 or 430px.

The problem is vertical. #rightPane collapses to 393 × 66, so every calendar view inside it measures 0 × 0 — dayView, weekGrid and monthGrid all render at zero size on a phone, in every tab. The empty band is just the page background showing through.

  tree pane        275 px
  controls strip    66 px
  EMPTY            ~440 px   <- nothing is here
  tab bar           73 px
                   ------
                   852 px

So on a phone you get the task tree and no calendar at all. Habits proves the layout can fill that space — it measures 373 × 207 and renders fine. It is specifically the calendar that never appears.
