1 The link works in Safari and IE but not Chrome. Chrome refuses a file:// navigation started from another app, and it treats a bare 127.0.0.1:8787 as a search. Safari does not. Stop relying on a local link. Publish the preview to the same https site as the planner — …/planner/preview/. One URL, works in every browser, nothing to keep running. ready
2 A login page, then the planner. The app has Connect GitHub / Sign in to sync, but nothing gates the planner itself. A proper sign-in page that hands off to the planner. One thing to know before you pick this: a login drawn in the page is a curtain, not a lock — anyone can read the file. Real protection means gating on the sync account you already have. I would build it that way. your call
3 Remove a date on a task; branches need no due date. There is a clear — a small × on the date chip in the tree. It is not in the Edit dialog, which is where you looked. Put Clear date in the Edit dialog next to the date, for tasks and branches alike. Clearing takes it off the calendar and leaves it in the tree, undated. Nothing is deleted. ready
4 Something I updated came back as missed today. Not yet reproduced — I need to see it happen on your data. My suspicion is a completed-for-today mark being read against the wrong day. Instrument it in the app so the next time it happens I can see which day it compared against, then fix the comparison. I would rather find it than guess. need your data
5 The clock sign should use the nice calendar. Two different controls: the due date opens the two-month picker, the alarm opens only the time spinner. Give the clock the same picker — date on the left, time on the right, one control everywhere. ready
6 The read section duplicates — top and bottom. Not reproduced yet. Typing in a tree title did not duplicate a row in my test, so it is something more specific to that item. Reproduce from your real data first. If it is what I think — a row drawn once by the tree and again by a later painter — the fix is one owner for that row. need your data
7 Split habits and routines. A routines engine already exists and routines already appear on the calendar. There is no UI for them — no way to see or set one up. A Routines tab beside Habits, top left of that pane, with the set-up living there. Both listed in the Location section. ready
8 Saving in focus mode flickers the other tabs. Almost certainly the same repaint I added for focus sessions — a save fires a change event and every pane redraws. Repaint only the pane you are looking at, not all of them. ready
9 In the journal, expanding a box snaps back when I stop typing. This one is mine. A repaint rebuilds the box from scratch, so your drag-resize — and the cursor — are thrown away. Never rebuild a field you are typing in. Update the page around it and leave the focused box alone. ready
10 A link in notes turns into plain words. Notes are stored and drawn as plain text, so a URL is just characters. Draw links as links while keeping what you typed exactly as typed. Clickable, still editable. ready
11 Movies shows SEP 26 ten times over. Could not reproduce — the export on this machine has no Movies task, so I am looking at stale data. A chip appended each time instead of replaced would do exactly this. I will reproduce it on your data and fix the redraw, not paper over the text. need your data
