1

Two tabs, and one lost your branches

data loss — approve

Which control: nothing to press — it happens by itself when two tabs of the planner are open.

"at work I had two tabs of the planner open, one only had Willingboro, Health, and family and the other had everything why was it missing"

now

The planner writes a delete record for anything that was in a tab a moment ago and is not there now. It cannot tell “you deleted this” from “this tab had not loaded it yet”. So a tab holding part of your planner records deletes for the branches it never had — and a delete is permanent and travels to every device.

would be

A delete is recorded only when this device actually removes something. A tab that never held a branch records nothing. And if many items disappear at once, the planner stops and asks instead of recording it silently.

where this stands

Found by reading the code, not guessed: syncTrack compares the tree against what this tab last saw and marks everything missing as deleted.

The check is written and runs first: two tabs, one with the whole planner, one with three branches — every branch must survive on both and in the cloud, and the partial tab must record no deletes. It runs against your live build before any fix.

This is the one I would fix first. It can lose work.
