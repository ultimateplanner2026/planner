the cause, measured, and how I fix it

You were right that something is on the calendar that should not be. It is not quite an item with no date — it is an item with no time, and the planner invents one.

tgMigrate, which runs once on your planner:

  morning  → 09:00
  afternoon → 13:00
  evening  → 18:00

and it writes that into the activity.
A block is a part of the day; a time is a point in it. Turning one into the other is a guess, and it was written into your data as though you had typed it. The item then left the All day row and appeared on the hour grid at a time nobody chose.

Measured on your build, an activity dated today with block=morning and no time:

buildtime storedwhere it is drawn
yours now09:00 (invented)on the hour grid
the fixnonethe All day row
The fix: the migration stops writing a time. The block is kept, and the item sits in the All day row until you give it a time yourself.
