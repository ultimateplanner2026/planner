the cause, measured, and how I fix it

First, the answer to your own question — "is it a routine or an activity?" It is both. Items like Bins out and Monday reset are a weekly repeating TASK with a routine record attached to it. That is why it behaves like an activity on the calendar and like a routine in the tracker.

What dragging one did before, measured: it moved the WHOLE SERIES. A task on Oct 4 and Oct 11 became Oct 5 and Oct 12. One late week would have silently changed your routine's day for good.

What you chose, and what is built:
• Drag it to another day and only THAT week moves. Next week is exactly where it always was.
• If you never do it, it rolls over by itself and keeps showing on today until you tick it off — so a missed Sunday is still in front of you on Tuesday without you having to move anything.
• It is shown once, not smeared across every day since it was due. One missed week is one row.
• Ticking it off stops the chase immediately.

Where this is stored: the planner already had a field called exceptions on every repeat rule that was created and then never read once. This uses a sibling of it, keyed by the date the occurrence was due, so the repeat rule itself is never touched.

The picture shows: NEXT WEEK, after dragging one occurrence forward. This is where the difference shows: on the left the whole series has shifted; on the right only the one week moved. (The extra chip on Mon 12 is the roll-over, because that moved occurrence was never ticked off.)
