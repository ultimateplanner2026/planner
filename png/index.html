my answer to your question

You asked whether it would interfere with a task you are in the middle of. It would, if it reloaded on its own — that is exactly the risk, and it is why I would not build it that way.

The safe shape is the one on card 2: the app notices, says so, and waits for you. Nothing is ever thrown away mid-sentence, and you still never have to wonder whether what you are looking at is current.
