how to use this page

Cards are numbered and the numbers never change — say 3 and 9 are wrong and I will know exactly which.

Each shot is cropped to the thing that changed, so what you are looking at is the change itself, not the whole screen.

1

Outdent no longer hides the branch

waiting

"when I edit indent backward it removes the visual of the branch it’s from"

was wrong

A rule files any dated, titled, top-level item with no children into “Unbranched”. Outdent a branch’s last child and the branch matched that shape.

now

The branch stays where you left it — and it is guarded by shape, so deleting the last child is covered too.
