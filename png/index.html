Below are five candidates, each one measured against your actual planner — not copied from another app. Nothing here is built. Tick the ones you want.

where this stands

1   Empty the deleted list — the strongest one

Measured in YOUR planner: 72 deleted items, the oldest from 6 Aug, and nothing ever clears them. The only way to remove them is Delete forever on each one — 144 buttons to press, one at a time.

What I would add: one line in Settings — Empty deleted items (72) — and an option to clear anything deleted more than 30 days ago by itself. Deleting is already undoable for 30 days, so nothing is at risk.

2   Week starts on Sunday or Monday

This is a gap, not a new feature. Your planner already stores this setting and already honours it in two places — there has simply never been a control for it. It is fixed on Sunday today.

What I would add: one toggle. Cheapest thing on this list by a wide margin.

3   How long a new task lasts

When you drop a task on the day grid it is drawn 30 minutes long — a number written into the code, not chosen by you. To change one you press the task and type a number into a box.

What I would add: New tasks last ___ minutes. Worth it only if 30 is usually wrong for you. If 30 is about right, skip this one — a setting you never open is clutter.

4   24-hour clock

Every time in the app is written as 9 AM / 2 PM, in six different places in the code. There is no 24-hour option.

What I would add: one toggle, Show times as 14:00. Only worth it if you prefer it — this is the one I would drop first.

5   The things you already have, but cannot reach

Three finished features sit in the app with no way in: local snapshots of your whole planner, a diagnostics screen, and rotate my sync key. You already said snapshot restore belongs on an owner page outside the app, so I am not proposing to put it back here — I am telling you it exists so nothing is hidden from you.

What I deliberately left off

Time zone — your planner has none; it uses whatever the computer says, which is the right answer on a work Mac and an iPhone both.
Auto-archive completed items — other apps have it; you have 2 completed items. It would solve nothing.
Text size and density — that is Visual formats wearing a different hat, and you just removed it.
Sign out — already there, under the ⋮ menu at the top right.

My honest recommendation

Take 1 and 2. One is a real mess in your own data; the other is a control that should have existed all along. The rest only earn a place if you want them — say the number and I will build it, say nothing and it stays off.
