THE RULES OF THIS BOARD — permanent, they never come off

  1. Your list goes up here first. Every item you raise becomes a card with a BEFORE picture, an AFTER picture and how I would fix it. Nothing is written into the planner before that.

  3. Approved is not live. A card you approve stays here as APPROVED until the build ships, so you can see what is queued.
  4. A card never leaves until you say remove. This board is the record of what you asked for, not a list of what I finished.
  5. Your words are kept, all of them — one line per thing you said. Your first quote stays at the top of the card forever; every later correction is its own dated line underneath, never merged into a paragraph, so you can check each one off. I never replace what you said with my summary.
  6. The AFTER is not one picture — it explains the new setup, step by step, in different pictures. What happens first, what happens next, what it looks like when it works. Never the same picture twice. If nothing is built yet, the card says so in plain words instead of pretending.
  7. Every question sits inside the problem it belongs to — never collected somewhere else. You read one card and answer it there.
  8. I raise what you have not thought of. Every card carries an I also thought of note: the thing your request does not decide — the crowded case, the phone, what happens to what you already have.
  9. I test it in Safari myself — before I call it done, and again after it goes live.
  10. Pictures, not paragraphs. Short words around the picture, never an essay above it.
  11. Every picture is half a page — including the step pictures. The BEFORE and AFTER are half each, and a step picture is the same size, not a thumbnail, because you have to see what is inside it. A shot of the whole browser takes the full width on its own row.
  12. EVERY APPROVED CARD IS SHOWN CONSOLIDATED — folded shut. The moment you approve one it collapses to a single line, so the board only ever shows you what still needs you. Press open beside its number to look at it again. This one is never skipped.
