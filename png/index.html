approvedhideopen

NOT LIVEAPPROVED

APPROVED — goes live when you say so

Where: the Edit window → Repeat → the frequency dropdown, when it sits near the bottom.

your words, kept in full — one line per thing you said

first said "Its okay but the popup comes out of the box when the monthly dropbox is at bottom of the edit, it must be within the edit layout."

the cause, measured, and how I fix it

Reproduced and measured. With the control near the dialog's bottom edge, the list ran 71px past the bottom of the Edit window and sat on the page behind it.

Why: the list kept itself inside the SCREEN, not inside the window it belongs to. It also had a fixed 120px minimum height, so even when it tried to fit, 120px was more than a short dialog had left.

The fix: a list opened from inside a dialog is bounded by that dialog. It shrinks and scrolls to fit, and if it would still reach past the bottom it lifts so its foot rests on the dialog's edge.

After: 71px over → 1px INSIDE, still scrollable, still usable.

The picture shows: the frequency list at the bottom of the Edit window, before and after.

The size you approved is unchanged — 12px text, 26px rows, your original colours. This card is only about where it is allowed to reach.

One thing worth knowing: when the dialog is very short the list becomes a small scrolling box rather than a tall one. That is deliberate — a list you scroll inside the window beats a list that spills behind it.
