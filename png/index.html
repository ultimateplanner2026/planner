So it is repeating tasks. They land on the day you dropped them, but their Due Date stays empty — which is the blank box in your picture.

Why. The planner has a rule for repeating tasks: moving one week’s occurrence must not move the whole series. So instead of writing a date it records "this occurrence moved from day A to day B". To do that it needs to know day A — the day the task was already showing on.

A task dragged out of the tree has no day A. It has never been on the calendar. The planner recorded the move anyway, filing it as "moved from null", and reported back that it had handled everything — so the step that writes the real date was skipped entirely. The task showed up on the day through that bogus record while its own date stayed empty.

The fix: if there is no day A, there is no occurrence to move, so the planner does the ordinary thing and writes the date of the day you dropped it on. Moving an occurrence of a repeat that is already on the calendar is unchanged — I tested that half too, because a fix that quietly switched the rule off would look identical in your picture.

This affected the day grid, the week grid, the month grid and the tree list at once, because all four ask that same question before writing a date.

The picture shows: ['two tasks, the same drag, your build: plain gets a date, REPEATING gets none', 'the repeating one still appears on the calendar - which is why it looks fine', 'on the fix both get the dropped day, and the bogus "null" record is gone', 'AND moving one occurrence of a repeat still works and does not move the series', 'proven to fail on build 2026-11-05 21:30 and pass on the fix']
